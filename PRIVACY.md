# Privacy — COACHWORK and DocketDiff

This notice covers buying and downloading two programs sold by DM11 Holdings. Questions: dm11holdings@gmail.com.

## COACHWORK Mark I

**The program.** What COACHWORK keeps on your computer, and what it does and does not send, is described in the README of the COACHWORK repository on GitHub ("What it does" and "What it does not do"). In short, from the program's own README: it keeps your records on your computer and has no function that sends your records over the internet.

**Paying.** Payment is handled by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us. Stripe keeps a record of your order in our Stripe account (for example the e-mail address you gave at checkout, what you bought, the amount and the date). We use it only to answer questions and make refunds, and keep it for seven years as accounting and tax records, unless you ask Link or Stripe to delete your data: Stripe then removes it from the records in our Stripe account.

**Downloading.** The program and the licence files are downloaded from GitHub. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that. We look only at how many times each file was downloaded; we do not receive who downloaded it.

**Writing to us.** If you e-mail us at dm11holdings@gmail.com, we keep your message to answer it.

**Our pages.** The pages that describe and sell these programs, and the page for each licence, are in our repositories on GitHub and are shown by GitHub. We add no analytics or tracking of our own to them, and we receive nothing about you from them. GitHub may keep its own records of each visit and uses cookies; its own privacy statement applies there: [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The payment page belongs to Stripe; its own terms and privacy statement apply there.

## DocketDiff

**The program.** DocketDiff runs on your computer. On the network it goes only to `https://data.transportation.gov`, to read the FMCSA tables named in the README and their descriptions, and it follows no redirect. The request text holds a table id, a date and a page position; it never holds your carrier numbers. As with any website, data.transportation.gov can see that your computer read the tables (its IP address and the time). DocketDiff does not send your carrier list, notes, decisions or transactions anywhere, and DM11 receives nothing from the program. It does not read phone numbers, addresses, names, insurance company names or policy numbers from the tables. Your carrier list, your decisions, your records and the receipt number and payment date you type for a licence stay in files on your computer.
